# On-Call Rotation

## Coverage
Each service has a primary and backup engineer. The weekly rotation starts Monday at 09:00 UTC and ends the following Monday at 09:00 UTC.

## Handoff
The outgoing primary records active alerts, maintenance, and known risks. The incoming primary acknowledges the handoff before the rotation changes.

## Responsibilities
The primary responds to pages and coordinates initial triage. The backup assists on request and automatically joins a declared P1.
