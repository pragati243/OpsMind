# SLA Definitions

## Availability targets
payments-api and auth-service target 99.95% monthly availability. search-index, notification-worker, billing-sync, and admin-portal target 99.9%.

## Incident targets
P1 acknowledgement is due within 10 minutes and mitigation within 60 minutes. P2 acknowledgement is due within 30 minutes and mitigation within four hours.

## Measurement
Availability excludes approved maintenance announced five business days in advance. Incident timing begins at the first verified monitoring event.
