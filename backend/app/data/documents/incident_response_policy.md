# Incident Response Policy

## Severity and declaration
Declare a P1 when a critical customer capability is unavailable, data integrity is at risk, or a security incident is suspected. The incident commander opens the incident channel and assigns a responder within ten minutes.

## Response expectations
Responders stabilize customer impact before investigating root cause. Do not make irreversible production changes without an approved change record. Provide stakeholder updates every 30 minutes for P1 incidents and every 60 minutes for P2 incidents.

## Closure
Close an incident only after customer impact has stopped, monitoring is stable, and the timeline contains the mitigation. P1 and P2 incidents require a postmortem.
