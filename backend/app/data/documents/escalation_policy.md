# Escalation Policy

## When to escalate
Escalate P1 incidents immediately to the incident commander. Escalate a P2 to the service owner if it remains unresolved for 60 minutes or requires service-specific expertise.

## Leadership notification
The operations lead notifies engineering leadership within 15 minutes of declaring a P1. Customer Support receives an approved summary before any external communication.

## Vendor escalation
Collect timestamps, request identifiers, impact scope, and current mitigation before engaging a vendor. The on-call engineer remains accountable while a vendor case is open.
